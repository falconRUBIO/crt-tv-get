CRT TV

for Amazon Fire TV Stick

Download CRT TV 0.5
  1. On your Fire Stick, install the Downloader app (orange icon, by AFTVnews) from the Amazon Appstore.
  2. Open Settings > My Fire TV > Developer Options > Install Unknown Apps and allow Downloader.
  3. Open Downloader and type: is.gd/crtapk
  4. Press Go, then choose Install when the download finishes.
  5. Open CRT TV from Your Apps & Channels.
